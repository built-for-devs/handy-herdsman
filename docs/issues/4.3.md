TITLE: 4.3 — Photo/media uploads
MILESTONE: M4 — Herd records portal
LABELS: devin,records
---
## Summary
Photo/attachment uploads on cattle profiles and at appointments, chronological and comparable over time.

## Spec references
- §10b (Photos & attachments)

## Requirements
- Polymorphic `media` (mediable = cattle | visit), with `uploaded_by` / `uploaded_role`, caption, `taken_at`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Clients can upload photos** to their cattle profiles (useful for for-sale listings) (§10b, 11.1).
- [ ] **Jeff can upload photos at an appointment** via the completion form — healing after dehorning, BCS comparison, injury documentation (§10b, 7.1).
- [ ] Photos attach to the animal and optionally to the specific visit; ordered chronologically for comparison (§10b).
- [ ] Soft-delete only.

## Mobile behavior
Camera capture / upload in one tap; large thumbnails; works on poor signal (queue upload).

## Config over code
Storage disk via filesystem config.

## Tests
- Upload attaches to cattle (and optionally visit); ordered by taken_at.

## Definition of done
Client + Jeff uploads, chronological, attached to animal/visit.
