MILESTONE: M3 — Clients, teams, onboarding

## Summary
Per-client messaging consent and channel preferences. Consent is separate from having a phone number; preferences group into 4 categories.

## Spec references
- §5.7 (Contact & consent model; channel preferences), §10b

## Requirements
- `consent_sms` (json) — separate, timestamped, **per category**.
- `channel_prefs` (json) — 4 categories × (email|text|both).
- `staff_channel_override` (json) + note.
- Categories + defaults + quiet hours from `config('reminders')`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **`consent_sms` is a SEPARATE, timestamped opt-in per category** — having their number is not permission to auto-text; **Jeff texting them as a human ≠ the app auto-texting** (§5.7).
- [ ] TCPA: **transactional consent ≠ promotional consent** — the consent record captures which categories they agreed to (§5.7).
- [ ] 4 categories, each email/text/both, with **at least one channel on per category** (clients can't zero out everything) (§5.7).
- [ ] Defaults: **cat.1 (time-sensitive) = both**; **cat.4 (seasonal/content) = email-only** unless opted in (TCPA-safe) (§5.7).
- [ ] **Staff override** per client (with a note) wins over client prefs; the client's own settings stay intact underneath (§5.7).
- [ ] **Quiet hours** (~8am–9pm from `config('reminders.quiet_hours')`); **on-call/emergency bypasses** quiet hours (§5.7, §10b).

## Mobile behavior
Toggle grid (4 categories × channel), large switches, clear consent copy; one-handed.

## Config over code
Categories, defaults, quiet hours from `config('reminders')`.

## Tests
- Cannot disable all channels in a category.
- SMS blocked without per-category consent.
- Staff override wins; underlying prefs preserved.

## Definition of done
Consent + 4-category prefs + staff override modeled per §5.7; resolution ready for M9.
