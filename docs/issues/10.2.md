## Summary
Conception-rate analytics, with BCS-vs-conception as the headline report.

## Spec references
- §5.6c (AI success rates)

## Requirements
- Compute conception rate from breedings → final preg-check outcomes (7.3), sliced by dimensions.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Conception rate by: overall, **sire, breed, client, cow vs heifer, protocol type (sync vs natural), season, and BCS at time of breeding** (§5.6c).
- [ ] **BCS vs conception is the HEADLINE report** — turns "fat cows don't breed well" into evidence Jeff can show a client (e.g. "BCS 7+ settled X% vs Y% at 5–6") (§5.6c).
- [ ] **BCS trend per animal over time** (improving or sliding) (§5.6c).
- [ ] Client value over time (revenue per client, repeat rate, dormancy) (§5.6c).
- [ ] Uses BCS captured on breeding visits (7.1) and final preg results (7.3).

## Mobile behavior
Simple, mobile-readable charts/numbers; the BCS-vs-conception view front and center.

## Config over code
Derived from records; no hardcoded thresholds beyond BCS scale.

## Tests
- Conception rate by sire/breed/cow-vs-heifer computed from sample data.
- BCS bucket comparison produces the headline metric.

## Definition of done
Conception analytics across dimensions; BCS-vs-conception headline; BCS trend; mobile-readable.
