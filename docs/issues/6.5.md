TITLE: 6.5 — On-call requests (heat / emergency)
MILESTONE: M6 — Booking & scheduling
LABELS: devin,booking
---
## Summary
On-call requests (standing heat, calving emergency) are submitted in the app and immediately page Jeff by SMS.

## Spec references
- §5.5, §10b (On-call), §3 (on-call heat)

## Requirements
- `oncall`-type bookings; immediate SMS alert to Jeff via sent.dm.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] On-call requests are **submitted IN the app** (system of record, keeps clients returning) (§10b).
- [ ] The app **immediately SMS-alerts Jeff** as the pager; he responds in the app (§10b).
- [ ] Rationale honored: standing-heat window ~6–12h and calving emergencies are minutes-to-hours — **notification delivery cannot be the weak link** (§10b).
- [ ] On-call bypasses availability/quiet hours (§10b, 6.3, 9.1).
- [ ] Heat breeding follows the AM/PM rule context (standing heat AM → breed PM; PM → next AM) (§3).

## Mobile behavior
One-tap "I have a standing heat / calving emergency"; minimal fields; instant confirmation.

## Config over code
SMS channel via config (sent.dm).

## Tests
- On-call submission dispatches an immediate SMS to Jeff.
- On-call ignores quiet hours + availability.

## Definition of done
In-app on-call with immediate SMS paging; bypasses availability/quiet hours.
