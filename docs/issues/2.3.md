## Summary
The SEO workhorse and the source nurture emails pull from.

## Spec references
- §5.3 (Education / blog).

## Requirements
- `posts` + `categories`. Index, pillar/category pages, individual posts.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Index, category/pillar pages, and post pages render from `posts`/`categories`.
- [ ] Pillars supported: heat detection, AI protocol, **nutrition (#1 AI-failure cause)**, health/testing, calving.
- [ ] Content is reusable by the nurture engine (§9.4).

## SEO
- [ ] Clean slugs, meta title/description, article schema, fast load.

## Mobile behavior
- [ ] Readable long-form on phone; fast on poor signal.

## Definition of done
Blog index/category/post live with SEO fundamentals.
